### `site/postgresql.md` 473, its clause alone

At the baseline: the bullet of line 473 of `site/postgresql.md`, in "What a method reads and throws".  Now: whole, on `site/reference/databases/postgresql/query-methods.md#method-results`.  Its clause "SqlSource is tested on Npgsql 8 and 10" is the source of the cell "8, 10" of "Driver versions tested" in the table of `#supported-versions`.  The bullet is here alone, for phase 19 to read beside the table.

```text
- **Every type that [the table](#type-table) gives a C# type is sent and read on Npgsql 8, 9 and 10**, as the C# type of the version's column, and SqlSource is tested on Npgsql 8 and 10.  `cube`, which Npgsql 8 and 9 do not read, is sent and read on Npgsql 10.  So is each type of [NodaTime](#nodatime), on each version with its `Npgsql.NodaTime`, and of [NetTopologySuite](#nettopologysuite), with its `Npgsql.NetTopologySuite`.
```

### `site/sqlserver.md` 28

At the baseline: the third bullet of "Connecting" of `site/sqlserver.md`, line 28.  Now: its first two sentences are a bullet under the table of `site/reference/databases/index.md#supported-versions`, and its third is the paragraph of `#azure-sql`.  Its link is retargeted from `site/reference/databases/`; its words are as below.

```text
- **SQL Server 2017 and later are supported**, and SqlSource is tested on 2017, 2019, 2022 and 2025.  An older server is [SQLSRC235](diagnostics.md#sqlsrc235).  Azure SQL Database and Azure SQL Managed Instance are accepted whatever version they report, and are not tested.
```
