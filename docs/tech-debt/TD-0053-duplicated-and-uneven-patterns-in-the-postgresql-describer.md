# TD-0053 - Duplicated and uneven patterns in the PostgreSQL describer

## Problem

A maintainability review of phase 3 on 2026-10-16 found these in `src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/` and `Describing/Capture/`.  No answer is wrong; each copy is one more place a change has to reach, and some are rules that already disagree.

1. **Six readers of quoted names.**  The quote skipper is written twice, [`PlanExpression`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/PlanExpression.cs)'s `Tokens.SkipQuoted` and [`PlanPartitions`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/PlanPartitions.cs)'s `Closing`; the `""` unescape three times, there and in [`OutputReference.Quoted`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/OutputReference.cs); and a quoted name is read again by [`PostgresSearchPath.Schemas`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSearchPath.cs) and [`DeclaredTypeText.TryReadQuoted`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/DeclaredTypeText.cs).  The bare-name rule is `OutputReference.Bare` and, inverted, [`PostgresTypeNames.IsBare`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresTypeNames.cs); a plan's name part is written in `PlanExpression` and in `PlanPartitions`, whose `WithoutAlias` is a third tokenizer of an `Output` entry.
2. **Five rules for a character that continues a word of the SQL.**  [`StatementShape`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/StatementShape.cs) and [`PostgresErrors`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresErrors.cs) hold the same `IsLetterOrDigit || _ || $`; [`OuterJoinGate`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/OuterJoinGate.cs) uses the lexer's rule, without `$`; `DeclaredTypeText` has no `$`; `StatementShape.PositionalParameter` adds anything above U+007F; `PlanExpression` is ASCII only.  `PostgresErrors.Undeclared`, `Uncast` and their `Pieces` helpers, about a hundred lines, are a word scanner of the SQL inside the class that maps failures.
3. **The user's SQL is lexed six or seven times a describe**, and the lex-or-throw block, with its message, is pasted in [`ParameterPlaceholders`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/ParameterPlaceholders.cs), `StatementShape` and `OuterJoinGate`: `Rewrite`, `RefusedKind`, `PositionalParameter`, `IsGated`, `NamesOldOrNew` and `AsWritten` in [`PostgresSession`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSession.cs), and `Rewrite` again in [`NpgsqlCheck`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/NpgsqlCheck.cs).
4. **A plan's node types and relationships are literals in four files.**  `"Append"`, `"Merge Append"`, `"SetOp"`, `"Recursive Union"`, `"Subquery Scan"`, `"ModifyTable"`, `"Subquery"`, `"InitPlan"`, `"Member"`, `"Outer"` and `"Inner"` are spelled in [`PlanInstances`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/PlanInstances.cs), `PlanPartitions`, [`NullabilityWalk`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/NullabilityWalk.cs) and [`NullabilityWalk.SetOperations`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/NullabilityWalk.SetOperations.cs), some as private constants of one file and literals of another.  `VisitedNode.NullExtends` is a string, `"none"` compared as a constant in `PlanInstances` and as a literal in `NullabilityWalk`.
5. **Tree helpers written three times.**  `All(PlanNode)` is in `PlanInstances`, `PlanPartitions` and [`PlanRelations`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/PlanRelations.cs); the `Subquery` child of a `Subquery Scan` is found in `PlanInstances.ChildOf` and `SetOperations.LeafBranchOf`; `PlanRelations.Of` and `PlanPartitions.MembersOf` end in the same distinct sort by schema and name.  `PlanInstances` is built by `MayAnswerFromBranches` and again by `NullabilityWalk.Run`, and the empty partitions dictionary is made three times.  `NullabilityWalk.Expressions.NullableAt` and `Preserved` open with the same depth guard and child filter.
6. **Hand-kept type tables.**  The ten types that carry a modifier are listed in [`ColumnFacets`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/ColumnFacets.cs), [`TypeModifiers`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/TypeModifiers.cs) and `PostgresTypeNames.InCatalog`; built-in OIDs are bare numbers in `PlanExpression.TypesByName` and [`SetOperationTypes.FamilyOf`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/SetOperationTypes.cs), which no test holds to `pg_type`.  [`PostgresTypes`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresTypes.cs) switches on `typtype` three times, in both `Build`s and in `PairsOf`, which must ask for exactly the pairs `Build` will look up; the "no row for type" lookup is `PostgresTypes.Row`, [`ColumnTypes.RowOf`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/ColumnTypes.cs) and [`PostgresColumns.FacetsOf`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresColumns.cs).
7. **`PostgresSession` mixes the flow of a describe with its call wrappers and builders**, about 870 lines.  The time-ask-log pattern of a call is written ten times, six as wrappers and four inline, with the call names, which are a contract with the captures, as bare literals.  `ColumnOrigin.MissingFrom` must run before `ColumnOrigin.Find`, and `MissingType` before `PostgresTypes.Build`, by statement order alone.  `WalkAsync` and `RunSetOperation` take seven parameters.  `VersionOf` is used only by `PostgresLive`.
8. **Small repeats.**  `ServerLines(error with { Position = null }, 0)` six times; "The answer to 'prepare' holds no types and no error." thrown twice; `$n` read as a number in four places; `Invariant(FormattableString)` in `NpgsqlCheck` and `PostgresTypeNames`; the `bool?` to `LogValue` conversion six times in [`PostgresEvents`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresEvents.cs), for want of `LogValue.Of(bool?)`.  In [`PostgresLive`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresLive.cs) the three catches of a driver's refusal are pasted in `CheckAsync` and `RefusalOfAsync`; the `oid[]` and the schema-and-name parameters are built twice each; `tables` and `types` bind by name and `relations`, `partitions` and `spellings` by position; and `OpenSql` and `SettingNames` are matched by index.
9. **Members only tests use.**  The one-argument `PlanInstances.Find`, `RowDescriptions.Resolve` and `BatchRewrites.Resolve`, and `WalkScope` without `ColumnTypes`.
10. **[`CaptureJson.Options`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Capture/CaptureJson.cs) can be changed by any caller** before its first use, though a change to it changes every fixture.

## Why it exists

The describer grew over six sub-phases and four follow-ups, each adding the reader or the call it needed beside the last; nothing was wrong enough to stop for.

## Impact

Low.  2 and 4 are where a fix to one copy leaves another behind: a word rule or a node type changed in one file and not the others gives a different answer in one step of the describe.  3 costs a few lexes of a short text.

## Proposed fix

1. A `PostgresIdentifiers` helper with `SkipQuoted`, `Unquote`, `IsBareName` and `IsNamePart`, and `PlanPartitions.WithoutAlias` on `PlanExpression`'s tokens.
2. One `IsWordCharacter`, and `PostgresErrors.Uncast` and its helpers moved beside `StatementShape`.
3. Lex once in `PostgresSession.DescribeAsync` and pass the lexemes down, or one `PostgresSql.Lex` that throws.
4. `PlanNodeTypes` and `PlanRelationships` constants, or predicates on `PlanNode`, and a `NullExtension` enum written as text only by `PostgresEvents`.
5. `PlanNode.Descendants()` and `PlanNode.SubqueryChild`, one sort of relation names, one cache of `PlanInstances` per walk, and a shared guard for `NullableAt` and `Preserved`.
6. A `BuiltInTypes` class with named OIDs and the set of types with a modifier, driving `PlanOutputTests`' check of the cast families; `PairsOf` derived from `Build`; `PostgresTypes.Row` everywhere.
7. One generic `AskAsync(method, request, live, logEvent)` and constants for the call names; `ColumnOrigin.FindAll` returning the origins or the missing message; `VersionOf` in `PostgresLive`.
8. `ServerLinesWithoutPosition`, one reader of a `$n`, `LogValue.Of(bool?)`, a `when (IsDriverRefusal(e))` filter, `OidArray` and `AddRelations` helpers, one parameter style, and `OpenSql` built from `SettingNames`.
9. Remove them, or say in each why a test needs it.
10. `MakeReadOnly()` in its initializer.

## Trigger

The next change to how a quoted name or a word of the SQL is read (1, 2), a new node type the walk follows (4), or a new call of the describer (7); otherwise before phase 6 adds a second engine's describer beside this one.
