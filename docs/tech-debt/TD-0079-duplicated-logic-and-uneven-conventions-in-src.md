# TD-0079 - Duplicated logic and uneven conventions in src

## Problem

The maintainability review of 2026-10-27 found these in `src/`.  Each is small alone; together they are rules written in several places, which a change must find every copy of.  Paths are under `src/`.

1. ~~**A type's usable files and their queries' settings are worked out in three places, and the rule lives in the emitter.**  [`TypeEmitter.FindUsableFiles`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/TypeEmitter.cs) is called by [`MethodPlanner`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Methods/MethodPlanner.cs) and [`TypeModels`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Models/TypeModels.cs), so two planners depend on an emitter, and the loop "each usable file, each query, `QuerySettings.Resolve`" is written in `TypeEmitter.SelectMembers`, `MethodPlanner.Resolve` and `TypeModels.Of`.  The check of models matched by index and name is in `TypeModels.Of` and `MethodPlanner.FindModels` both.~~  Fixed: [`UsableQueries`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/UsableQueries.cs) holds `FindUsableFiles` and `Of`, which gives each query of the usable files with its indices and settings; `TypeEmitter`, `TypeModels` and `MethodPlanner` call it, and the match of models by index and name is `FileModels.Find`.
2. ~~**Binary searches are written out by hand ten times.**  Beside [`SortedSearch`](../../src/SqlSource/SortedSearch.cs): `SortedKeyedList`, `TypeOverrideMap.LowerBound` (TD-0063), [`ColumnTypeMap`](../../src/SqlSource/Settings/ColumnTypes/ColumnTypeMap.cs), `SqlBlockText.FindRun`, `FileModels.Find`, `MethodPlanner.FindAssignment`, `SqlPath.Seek`, [`ConverterSet`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Converters/ConverterSet.cs) and [`ModelAssignment`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Models/ModelAssignment.cs).  `ColumnTypeMap` also copies `TypeOverrideMap`'s `Order` and `ComparePart`, though its key is an ordinary struct that `SortedSearch` could take.~~  Fixed: `SortedSearch` offers a search by a probe, `IndexOf`, and a lower bound, `LowerBound`, and every search of a sorted list in `src/` goes through it but `TypeOverrideMap.LowerBound`, whose key is a `ref struct` (TD-0063).  The two maps share their order, `SortedKeyedList.Order`, and the comparison of a part, `TypeKeyText.ComparePart`.
3. ~~**The two describers mark their own errors differently.**  PostgreSQL throws `new NpgsqlException(text)` in `PostgresConnection` and knows its own by matching the message against `PostgresConnection.OwnTexts`, a list kept by hand with no test that every thrown text is in it; SQL Server throws `SqlServerConnection.UnusableException`, which needs no list.~~  Fixed: PostgreSQL throws `PostgresConnection.UnusableException`, which `PostgresLive.AnswerAsync` answers as it is; its texts are in `Postgres/ConnectionErrors`, and `PostgresConnectionTests` fails for an `NpgsqlException` the describer makes itself.
4. ~~**Four engine-neutral connection texts are written in both describers**: `SessionRefused`, `ConnectTimedOut`, `NoAnswerInTime` and `LostConnection`, in `Describing/Postgres/ConnectionErrors.cs` (`LostConnection` in `PostgresConnection.cs`) and `Describing/SqlServer/ConnectionErrors.cs`.  Each builds the "unreachable" text with the socket's error code, and only SQL Server's drops a meaningless `: Success`.~~  Fixed: [`Describing/Describers/ConnectionTexts`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Describers/ConnectionTexts.cs) holds the four and `Unreachable`, which drops `: Success` for both engines.
5. ~~**Parameter names are compared, ordinal ignoring case, inline at about 25 sites** with no named comparer, where database names have `DatabaseName.Comparer` and paths `SqlPath.Comparer`: `SqlParameterList`, `SqlMarkerScope`, `QueryTyper`, the tool's `EntryBuilder`, `NpgsqlCheck`, `ParameterPlaceholders`, `SqlServerErrors`, `SqlServerSession`, `SqlServerSession.Builders` and ten in `ParameterCopies`.~~  Fixed: [`SqlParameterName`](../../src/SqlSource/Parsing/SqlParameterName.cs) has `Comparer`, `Comparison` and `IndexOf` over a query's parameters, which every match of a parameter by its name uses, `SqlDeclaredVariables.Holds`, `UncastParameters` and `PostgresErrors.CannotBind` included.
6. **In nine key switches of the sidecar reader, `default:` stands for the last key of the array.**  In [`SidecarReader`](../../src/SqlSource/Snapshot/Reading/SidecarReader.cs) (six) and [`SidecarTypeReader`](../../src/SqlSource/Snapshot/Reading/SidecarTypeReader.cs) (three), a key added after it without a `case` of its own is read as that key, and nothing catches it.  `SidecarTypeReader.ReadOther` writes every key as a `case`.  `SidecarTypeReader` also relies on `BindName` being the last key so that its bits line up for `Require`, which only a comment holds.
7. ~~**SQL Server's naming rules are copied between the generator and the tool**, against `src/SqlSource.Tool/AGENTS.md`'s rule that the tool shares the generator's code: `SqlServerParameterBinder.Part` brackets a name only where needed and the tool's `SqlServerLive.Bracketed` always does, and the CLR system types `hierarchyid`, `geography` and `geometry` are listed in `SqlServerParameterBinder`, `SqlServerTypeMap` and the tool's `SqlServerTypes`.~~  Fixed: [`SqlServerNames`](../../src/SqlSource/SqlServerNames.cs) holds both bracketed forms, `Bracketed` for T-SQL text, where a keyword needs brackets, and `UdtPart` for a `UdtTypeName`, which SqlClient only splits at dots, and the CLR types of `sys`; the generator and the tool use it.
8. ~~**The parameter binders have two shapes.**  PostgreSQL's is a subclass in a file of its own; SQL Server's, `SqlServerParameterBinder`, sits at the end of `ParameterBinder.cs` and forwards to the static `SqlServerBinding.Append`.  `PostgresParameterBinder.AppendValue` copies `ParameterBinder.AppendConverted` but for a cast and the text for null, and the same seven-parameter `Append` signature is written five times.~~  Fixed: [`SqlServerParameterBinder`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/SqlServerParameterBinder.cs) is a subclass in a file of its own, as PostgreSQL's is; `ParameterBinder.AppendConverted` takes the cast and the text for null, and both binders call it; and every `Append` takes a [`BindingContext`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/BindingContext.cs) and the parameter.
9. ~~**`PostgresTypeMap.AsWritten` is a helper of C# type text for both engines that lives in PostgreSQL's map**, called from `ConversionPlanner` (11 times), `ConverterSet`, `ModelCollector` and `QueryTyper`, and named in three messages' comments of `SqlDiagnostics`.~~  Fixed: it is [`ClrTypeName.AsWritten`](../../src/SqlSource/Settings/ClrTypes/ClrTypeName.cs), beside `KeywordTextOf`, and the comments of `SqlDiagnostics` name it there.
10. ~~**A file's engine is found three ways**: `MethodPlanner.EngineOf` gives a `CodeGenEngines`, `SqlDescribableDialects.EngineOf` a `DatabaseEngine`, and `QueryTyper` decides by the type's class.  `MethodPlanner.Plan` is given the one and works out the other for the same file, and the driver check after it is a cascade of booleans.~~  Fixed: `MethodPlanner` finds the engine with `SqlDescribableDialects.EngineOf` alone, skips a file of a dialect that cannot be described, and asks `DatabaseEngine.ToCodeGenEngines()` and `MethodDrivers.Allows(engine)`.  `QueryTyper` still picks a map by the type's class, since a type of an engine this version does not know has no map.
11. ~~**The method planner passes the same per-query values in long lists**: `MethodPlanner.Plan` takes nine, `TryReadInput` and `TryReadOutput` eight each, and `ConversionPlanner.Plan` eight, from which it builds `Decision` three times, while the caller's `Reading` already holds the engine, the place and the problems.~~  Fixed: a `QueryContext` carries a query's values through `MethodPlanner`, and `ConversionPlanner.Plan` takes a [`ConversionContext`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Methods/ConversionContext.cs) whole, from which `Decision` and its element's `OfElement()` are built.
12. ~~**[`MappedTypeResolver`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/MappedTypes/MappedTypeResolver.cs) is about 880 lines with two jobs**: resolving a name, in a `ResolveType` of about 160 lines, and checking generic constraints (`Construct`, `MeetsConstraints`, `MeetsFlags`, `Converts`, `Substitute` and the rest, about 250 lines).~~  Fixed: the check of generic constraints is [`GenericConstraints`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/MappedTypes/GenericConstraints.cs), and `ResolveType` calls `LookUp`, `ResolveArguments` and `CanBeUsed` for the lookup of the name, its arguments and the checks of its use.
13. ~~**[`QueryMethodWriter`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/QueryMethodWriter.cs) is about 960 lines** that write the signature and its documentation, the SQL, the command, the run, the reading of rows (`AppendVectorChecks` to `AppendGetFieldValue`), the naming of locals (`Locals`), and the split of a streaming method into its checks and a static local iterator (`AppendBody`, `AppendArguments` and `AppendCallArguments`).~~  Fixed: the reading of a row is [`RowReaderWriter`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/RowReaderWriter.cs), and the naming of locals [`QueryMethodLocals`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/QueryMethodLocals.cs).
14. **A dead member**: `SelectStatement.CteNames` (`src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/Proofs/SelectStatement.cs`) is read only by `SelectStatementTests`; the same list goes to `SelectBody.Read` on its own.
15. **Mutable statics**: five `internal static readonly Dictionary` fields of `Settings/TypeOverrides/TypeKeyNames.cs`, which only `TypeKeys` reads and nothing writes.
16. ~~**The two describers lay out their folders differently**: PostgreSQL's table matcher, nullability, declared types and placeholders are in `Postgres/Columns/`, `Postgres/Statements/` and `Postgres/Types/`, where SQL Server's equivalents sit at `SqlServer/`'s root.~~  Fixed: SQL Server's mirror PostgreSQL's, with `SqlServerColumns` and `SqlServerTableMatcher` in `SqlServer/Columns/`, `DeclaredTypeText` and `ParameterCopies` in `SqlServer/Statements/`, and `SqlServerTypes` in `SqlServer/Types/`.

## Why it exists

Each grew where the feature that needed it was written, one phase at a time, and a second engine was added beside the first rather than over a shared base.  The review records them and does not refactor, so that its pull request stays a review.

## Impact

Low.  A key added to the sidecar without a `case` of its own is read as the last key of its array, and nothing catches it (item 6); and a dead member and dictionaries that nothing stops a later change from writing invite mistakes (items 14 and 15).

## Proposed fix

One item at a time, each in its own pull request, with no change to what is generated:

1. ~~Move `FindUsableFiles` and a `UsableQueries.Of(TypeQueries)` that yields each query with its file, indices and settings into `Generation/`, and call it from the three places.~~  Done.
2. ~~Make `SortedSearch` offer a probe-based search and a lower bound, route every search but `TypeOverrideMap`'s through it, and share `Order` and `ComparePart` between the two maps.~~  Done.
3. ~~Give PostgreSQL an `UnusableException` caught in `PostgresLive.AnswerAsync`, delete `OwnTexts` and `IsOwn`, and keep its texts in `Postgres/ConnectionErrors`.~~  Done.
4. ~~A `Describers/ConnectionTexts` with the four texts and one `Unreachable(prefix, SocketException)`.~~  Done.
5. ~~`SqlParameterName.Comparer` and `Comparison`, and one `IndexOf` over a query's parameters, used at every site.~~  Done.
6. A `case` for every key and a `default` that does nothing, as `ReadOther` has, or a test that each key array has a `case` for each key.
7. ~~One `SqlServerNames` in the generator, with the bracketing rule and the CLR system types, which the tool uses.~~  Done, with both bracketed forms, since T-SQL and SqlClient read a name differently.
8. ~~Move `SqlServerBinding`'s body into `SqlServerParameterBinder.cs`, let `AppendConverted` take the cast and the text for null, and bundle the builder, indent, command, parameters and names into one record.~~  Done, the parameter's local in the record too, since it is the same for every parameter of a method.
9. ~~Move `AsWritten` to `Settings/ClrTypes/ClrTypeName` or a helper of its own.~~  Done.
10. ~~One `EngineOf`, `CodeGenEngines.Of(DatabaseEngine)` and `MethodDrivers.Allows(engine)`.~~  Done, the mapping as `DatabaseEngine.ToCodeGenEngines()`, since an enum has no static method.
11. ~~A record of a query's context, and `Decision` or `Reading` passed to `ConversionPlanner` whole.~~  Done.
12. ~~Extract `GenericConstraints.cs` from `MappedTypeResolver`, and split `ResolveType`.~~  Done.
13. ~~Move the reading of rows to a `RowReaderWriter.cs`, and `Locals` to a file of its own.~~  Done, `Locals` as `QueryMethodLocals`.
14. Drop `SelectStatement.CteNames` and test through `Body`.
15. Expose the dictionaries as `IReadOnlyDictionary`.
16. ~~Decide whether the two describers' folders mirror each other, and move the other's files to match.~~  Done.

## Trigger

A new key of the sidecar, a change to `SelectStatement` or `TypeKeyNames`, or the next maintainability review.
