## Why it exists

Items 1 to 20 come from the first sweep of `src/`, items 21 to 28 from the sweep of the describers' sub-folders.  The bug-report bundles were built in four sub-phases by sessions working in parallel, each engine's renaming beside the other's, and each reached for the code it needed where it stood.  The older items grew one command or one form at a time.  The review records them and does not refactor, so that its pull request stays a review.

## Impact

Low each, Medium together: items 1, 2, 3, 8, 9, 10, 13, 14, 21 and 23 are copies that a change to one side leaves wrong without a failing test, where the renaming's safety, or a describer's answer, depends on the two sides agreeing; items 16 to 19 make the run's code harder to change.
