# TD-0073 - Duplicated and uneven patterns in `src/`

## Problem

A maintainability review of `src/` on 2026-10-22 found these.  Most come from the SQL Server describer, which phase 6 built beside the PostgreSQL one by copying its shape.  No behaviour is wrong; each copy is one more place a change has to reach, and two of them already disagree.

1. **The two describer sessions copy the same scaffolding.**  `OpenAsync` in [`PostgresSession`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSession.cs) and [`SqlServerSession`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerSession.cs) is about forty lines each with one body: make the connection, ask `open`, report its error, check the floor, dispose on failure.  `ServerOf`, `AskAsync` and the `Failed` shorthand are copied too, in [`PostgresSession.Builders.cs`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSession.Builders.cs) and [`PostgresSession.Calls.cs`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSession.Calls.cs) against `SqlServerSession.cs` and [`SqlServerSession.Calls.cs`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerSession.Calls.cs).  `SqlServerSession.cs`, about 600 lines, holds the builders that its sibling keeps in a `.Builders` part.
2. **The errors of both engines are built twice.**  `Open` (`SQLSRC224`), `VersionNotSupported` (`SQLSRC235`) and `DeclaredType` (`SQLSRC228`) are in both [`PostgresErrors`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresErrors.cs) and [`SqlServerErrors`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerErrors.cs), and so are the texts `NoSuchType` and `NotATypeName`; SQL Server keeps the second in its [`DeclaredTypeText`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/DeclaredTypeText.cs).
3. **A driver's exception becomes an answer in two ways.**  [`PostgresLive`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresLive.cs) writes `catch (NpgsqlException)` and its answer in twelve methods; [`SqlServerLive`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerLive.cs) routes every call through one `AnswerAsync`.
4. **The log's events repeat each other, and name one thing two ways.**  `Column` and `TableMatch` are in both [`PostgresEvents`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresEvents.cs) and [`SqlServerEvents`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerEvents.cs), which build the map of an origin and a table nine times between them.  The verdict of `table-match` is the member `result` for PostgreSQL and `tableMatch` for SQL Server; a member is never renamed, so the split stays unless one event gains the other's name beside its own.
5. **A version's major is read by three rules.**  `SqlClientProbe.LeadingInteger` in [`SqlClientProbe`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/SqlClientProbe.cs) gives 0 with no leading digits, `MajorVersionOf` in `PostgresSession.Builders.cs` gives null, and `IsBelowTheFloor` in `SqlServerSession.cs` parses up to the first dot.
6. **A parameter's prefix is stripped in nine places.**  From a lexeme: [`SqlParameterList`](../../src/SqlSource/Parsing/SqlParameterList.cs) twice, [`SqlDeclaredVariables`](../../src/SqlSource/Parsing/SqlDeclaredVariables.cs), [`ParameterCopies`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/ParameterCopies.cs), [`ParameterPlaceholders`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Statements/ParameterPlaceholders.cs) and [`UncastParameters`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Statements/UncastParameters.cs).  From a name the server reported: `Bare` in `SqlServerSession.cs`, `ParameterCopies` and [`NpgsqlCheck`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/NpgsqlCheck.cs).
7. **What a character of a T-SQL name is has three copies, and one disagrees.**  `IsWordCharacter` in `SqlDeclaredVariables` and `IsNamePart` in `SqlServerErrors` take a letter, a digit or `_@#$`; `IsNext` in SQL Server's `DeclaredTypeText` takes ASCII only.  PostgreSQL's rule is shared, as `QuoteReader.IsNameCharacterOfPostgreSql`.
8. **The system schemas are private constants in eight files, and `sys` is compared two ways.**  `pg_catalog` is in [`TypeKeys`](../../src/SqlSource/Settings/TypeOverrides/TypeKeys.cs), [`PostgresTypeMap`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/PostgresTypeMap.cs), [`PostgresSearchPath`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/PostgresSearchPath.cs), [`PostgresBindNames`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Types/PostgresBindNames.cs) and [`PostgresTypeNames`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Types/PostgresTypeNames.cs); `sys` in `TypeKeys`, [`SqlServerTypeMap`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/SqlServerTypeMap.cs) and [`SqlServerTypes`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerTypes.cs), named `SystemSchema` in some and `Catalog` in others.  `SqlServerTypeMap.IsSystemSchema` compares `sys` ordinally and `SqlServerTypes` ignoring case.
9. **PostgreSQL's types kept the plain names that SQL Server's siblings prefix.**  `DriverColumn` against `SqlClientColumn`, `OverrideOf` against `SqlServerOverrideOf` (which take a type and a key), `FileTypesInput.Probe` against `SqlClientProbe`, `TypeKeys.TryNormalize`, `Names`, `Aliases` and `KnownNames` against their `SqlServer` siblings, and `TableMatcher` against `SqlServerTableMatcher`.
10. **The two driver probes are reported by hand.**  [`NpgsqlProbe`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/NpgsqlProbe.cs) and `SqlClientProbe` have the same members under different `Version` types, and `ReportDriver` in [`SqlSourceGenerator`](../../src/SqlSource/SqlSourceGenerator.cs) takes seven parameters, three of them flags, from two near-identical callers.
11. **The order of the table match's checks is written twice.**  [`TableMatcher`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Columns/TableMatcher.cs) and [`SqlServerTableMatcher`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerTableMatcher.cs) each spell out the format's six checks; each engine's tests pin its own, and nothing ties the two together.
12. **`SqlServerSession` reads an `Outcome` its own way.**  It tests `Failure is { }` and then writes `Value!` in five places, where every other consumer tests `Succeeded`, whose `MemberNotNullWhen` needs no `!`.
13. **Small copies.**  `QueryTyper.IndexOf` in [`QueryTyper`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/QueryTyper.cs) and `ModelReader.IndexOfQuery` in [`ModelReader`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Models/ModelReader.cs) are one search; `InChain<T>` is in both engines' `ConnectionErrors`; [`PostgresSql.Lex`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Statements/PostgresSql.cs) and [`SqlServerSql.Lex`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/SqlServer/SqlServerSql.cs) differ by their rules alone; the two `DeclaredTypeText` readers differ in shape, PostgreSQL's a `TryRead` that gives no reason and SQL Server's an `Outcome` with one; and the generated file's header and the indent are constants of [`TypeEmitter`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/TypeEmitter.cs), [`ModelEmitter`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/ModelEmitter.cs) and [`MethodWriter`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Emitting/MethodWriter.cs) each.
14. **Four types do several jobs in over 550 lines.**  [`PlanExpression`](../../src/SqlSource.Tool/Describing/Postgres/Walk/PlanExpression.cs), about 1,000 lines besides its `.Forms` part, is a tokenizer, a reader of forms and the proofs of types; [`MappedTypeResolver`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Types/MappedTypeResolver.cs), about 800, holds 120 lines on the ids of obsolete and experimental warnings; `TypeKeys`, about 850, holds both engines' tables, the grammar of a key and the decoding of a Unicode escape; [`ModelCollector`](../../src/SqlSource/Generation/Models/ModelCollector.cs), about 560, holds 110 lines of the text of a conflict's summary.

## Why it exists

Phase 6 built the SQL Server describer beside a PostgreSQL one that had grown alone, and copied its shape where the engines agree so that each describer could be read on its own.  The large types grew one sub-phase at a time.

## Impact

Low.  A change to how a session opens, an error is worded, an event is written or a name is read reaches two or three places, and items 4, 7 and 8 show copies that have already drifted.

## Proposed fix

1. A base or helper of `Describing/Describers/` for opening a session, asking a call and building `ServerInfo`; move the builders of `SqlServerSession` to a `.Builders` part, as its sibling has.
2. Builders of `SQLSRC224`, `235` and `228` and the shared texts in `Describing/DescribeErrors.cs`, taking the engine and its help.
3. An `AnswerAsync(call, errorOf, failed)` in `PostgresLive`, as `SqlServerLive` has.
4. A shared builder of the column and table-match members; write `tableMatch` beside `result` in PostgreSQL's event, or record the split in `docs/architecture/tool.md`.
5. One `LeadingMajor` in the generator, used by the three.
6. `SqlLexeme.ParameterName` and one `WithoutPrefix` in `Parsing/`.
7. One `IsNameCharacterOfSqlServer` in `Parsing/Quoting/`, and decide whether a declared type's name may hold a letter that is not ASCII.
8. One constant per engine in the generator, and one `IsSystemSchema` for `sys`, ignoring case or not on purpose.
9. Prefix the PostgreSQL side (`Npgsql`, `Postgres`), updating the AGENTS.md files that name these types.
10. An interface over the two probes, so that `ReportDriver` takes the probe.
11. One ordered list of checks that takes each engine's predicates.
12. Test `Succeeded`.
13. One helper each; give PostgreSQL's `DeclaredTypeText` the `Outcome` shape and a reason; the emitters' constants in one place.
14. Split each along the seams named.

## Trigger

A third describer, which would copy each of 1 to 4 and 11 again; or a change that touches two copies of one item.
